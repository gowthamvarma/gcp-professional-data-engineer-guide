# Product Guidelines - GCP Professional Data Engineer Study Guide

## Tone & Voice
The guide adopts a **Conversational & Peer-to-Peer** tone. It should feel like a collaborative study session between a mentor and a learner. Use inclusive language (e.g., "Let's look at...", "You'll want to remember...") to make complex technical topics approachable and engaging.

## Content Structure & Clarity
- **Beyond Bullets:** While checklists are useful, every concept must be supported by a clear, concise description. Avoid "bullet-point soup" by providing context and explaining the *why* behind the facts.
- **Deep-Dive Links:** Every major concept should be accompanied by granular links to the specific section of the official Google Cloud documentation for those who want to explore further.

## Visual Standards & Callouts
To ensure a high-signal reading experience, use a combination of emojis and bold text-based callouts:
- 💡 **PRO TIP:** Strategic advice for the exam.
- ⚠️ **WARNING:** Common pitfalls or easily confused services.
- 🚀 **FAST FACT:** Key metrics or "hard" facts to memorize (e.g., limits, latencies).
- 🛠️ **BEST PRACTICE:** Architectural recommendations for real-world scenarios.

## Sample Question Standards
Sample questions must challenge the learner at multiple levels of depth:
1.  **Direct Fact-Check:** Validating basic knowledge of service features and limits.
2.  **Situational Analysis:** Applying knowledge to choose the best service for a specific use case.
3.  **Architectural Design:** Evaluating complex requirements (cost, latency, scale) to design an end-to-end solution.

Each question must be followed by a **detailed explanation** that clarifies why the correct answer is right and why the distractors are incorrect or sub-optimal.

## Markdown & Formatting
- Use clear, hierarchical headings (`#`, `##`, `###`).
- Use horizontal rules (`---`) to separate major services.
- Use code blocks for SQL, CLI commands, or YAML snippets.
- Ensure all links are descriptive (e.g., `[BigQuery Partitioning Docs](...)`).
